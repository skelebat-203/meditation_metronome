Privacy policy: Meditation Metronome

Last updated: 8 November 2026

In short

Meditation Metronome does not collect, send or share any data about you.

What the app keeps

If you save your settings, such as session length or the tick sound, they are kept on your phone only. They never leave it. You can remove them by clearing the app's storage or removing the app.

What the app does not do

  • It does not connect to the internet. It has no permission to do so.
  • It has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.
  • It does not ask for any phone permissions.
  • It does not share data with anyone, because it has none to share.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions? Open an issue on the project's GitHub page: github.com/skelebat-203/meditation_metronome/issues.
