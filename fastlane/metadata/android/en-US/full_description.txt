Meditation Metronome helps you sit still and keep track of time. It plays a soft tick once each second to help you stay with your breath.

What it does:

* Metronome: one tick each second. Pick a soft click, a wood block or a bell.
* Calm music: pick a track or let the app choose one.
* Images: a slow slideshow of calm pictures.
* Session length: set how long to sit. A chime plays at the end.
* Marks: a soft chime at set times, so you know how far along you are.
* Timer: show the time that has passed or the time that is left.
* Keep the screen awake while you sit.

Each part can be turned on or off. You can save your settings or use them just for one session.

Your privacy:

* Works fully offline. The app never connects to the internet.
* No ads and no tracking.
* No permissions needed.

Music and image credits are listed in the app.

Please note: the session stops when you leave the app or turn off the screen. Playback in the background is planned for a later version.
